Department Head and Endowed Faculty Chair, Department of Engineering, James Madison University

James Madison University (JMU) invites applications for the position of Department Head and Endowed Faculty Chair of Engineering. This is a tenured, 12-month appointment at the rank of Professor or Associate Professor. A recent $1.5 million gift establishing the endowed chair provides significant resources and flexibility for the Department Head to pursue a broad range of initiatives aligned with the Department’s needs, opportunities, and the successful candidate’s interests.

Our ABET-accredited Department of Engineering consists of 18 instructional faculty and 6 staff members serving approximately 750 students, with about 120 graduates each year. The Department offers a B.S. in Engineering, and students may choose to pursue concentrations in Electro-Mechanical Systems, Geo-Environmental Systems, or Civil & Environmental Systems. As an undergraduate-focused engineering program within a large public university, we combine the close faculty-student interaction and small class sizes often associated with smaller programs with substantial facilities, staffing, and resources. Our project-based curriculum gives students significant responsibility for their own work in courses, laboratories, research, and design projects, creating a highly hands-on learning environment. Students have access to a fully staffed machine shop, fabrication laboratory, digital prototyping facilities, and dedicated capstone and project workspaces. Research, project, and instructional infrastructure includes more than $2 million in tools, instrumentation, and equipment located in over 30,000 square feet of dedicated engineering laboratory, fabrication, research, and project space.

02 — The program

Curriculum

One ABET-accredited B.S. in Engineering — 129 credits — combining a 42-credit engineering core with math & science, a seven-credit practicum-through-capstone spine (Practicum I → Practicum II/Project I → Practicum III/Project II → Capstone) that produces the work in the next section, and 15 credits of electives that can be taken freely or assembled into one of three named concentrations — Civil & Environmental Systems, Geo-Environmental Systems, or Electro-Mechanical Systems. Full admission to the major is a competitive gate at the sophomore level, built on four prerequisite courses and a written statement.

The following year's team built Sirius I, a fiberglass high-power rocket with a triple-deployment recovery system and an extra parachute to stabilize an unusually heavy nose cone. Portable back-pain relief devices

Jun 17, 2026

Two capstone teams advised by Dr. Karim Altaii each built a portable spinal-traction device as an alternative to bulky clinic equipment — a pneumatic traction chair and a mechanical crank-driven design — and presented both at the 2026 Systems and Information Engineering Design Symposium at UVA. "The best part was the freedom — being given a problem and designing a solution from scratch." — Marvin Fuentes, team lead.

An accessible path to the ocean

Jul 2025 · published Sep 17, 2025

Engineering and ISAT students partnered with Punta Leona Beach Club and Nature Resort in Costa Rica to design a beach wheelchair — PVC frame, oversized sand wheels, breathable mesh seating — built to be locally maintainable and reproducible, plus a lighter three-wheel second prototype. "This wasn't just a prototype anymore — it was real." — Daniel Turner, team member.
